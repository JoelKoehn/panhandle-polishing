Privacy Policy

Panhandle Polishing · effective 2026-11-07

Who we are

Panhandle Polishing ("we", "us") runs the website https://panhandlepolishing.com. This page explains what information we collect when you visit the site or contact us, what we do with it, and the choices you have.

What we collect

Information you give us. When you fill in a form on this site, or call, text or email us, we collect what you tell us: your name, phone number, email address, whether you want shop or mobile service, your location, any photos you upload and anything you write in your message.

Information collected automatically. Like most websites, we see which pages are visited, what kind of device and browser is used, roughly where visitors are, and how they found the site.

How we use it

We use your information to reply to you and to run our business. We also use visit information to understand which pages work and which are confusing, so we can improve the site.

Who sees it

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with companies that help us run the website, and only for that purpose:

  • Koehn Social Strategies, the company that built and looks after this website. Messages sent through our forms are delivered to them as well as to us.
  • Netlify, which hosts the website and delivers form messages.
  • Google, through Google Analytics, which measures visits to the site.
  • Microsoft, through Microsoft Clarity, described below.
  • Anyone the law requires us to give it to.

Analytics and cookies

We use Google Analytics, which uses cookies and similar technology to count visits and see which pages and links are used. Google handles that information under its own privacy policy.

We also use Microsoft Clarity, a free tool that records how visitors use our pages (where they click, how far they scroll and which pages they view) so we can find and fix confusing pages. Clarity is set not to record what you type into forms. Clarity handles this information under its own privacy policy.

You can limit this by setting your browser to block cookies and trackers, or by using the opt-out tools at optout.aboutads.info. The site still works without them.

Calls and texts

If you give us your phone number we may call or text you about your request. Tell us to stop and we will. We do not sell or share your number for anyone else to market to you.

How long we keep it

We keep messages for as long as we need them to deal with your request and keep ordinary business records, and then delete them.

Your choices

You can ask us what we hold about you, ask us to correct it or delete it, and ask us to stop contacting you. Contact us using the details below and we will respond promptly.

Children

Our site is for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.
